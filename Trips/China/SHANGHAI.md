# Shanghai One-Day Itinerary (Based on Video Analysis)
 
A full-day itinerary covering Pudong, Bund, shopping districts, People's Square, Yu Garden, rooftop viewpoints, and Indian dining in Shanghai.
 
---
 
## 08:00 AM – Start in Pudong
 
Begin the day in the Pudong financial district and walk towards **Dongchang Road Ferry Terminal**. The riverside promenade is clean, green, and ideal for a morning walk.
 
### Highlights
- Huangpu River waterfront
- Pudong skyline views
- Relaxed morning atmosphere
 
---
 
## 09:00 AM – Huangpu River Ferry Ride
 
Take the public ferry from **Dongchang Road Pier** to **Jinling Road Ferry Station** near the Bund.
 
### Ferry Details
- Cost: 2 Yuan
- Duration: Approximately 10 minutes
- Frequency: Every 30 minutes
 
### Why Take It?
This is one of the cheapest and most scenic ways to experience Shanghai's skyline. The ferry offers excellent views of:
 
- Shanghai Tower
- Shanghai World Financial Center
- Jin Mao Tower
- The Bund
- Huangpu River
 
**Tip:** Take the ferry both ways for views from each side of the river.
 
---
 
## 10:00 AM – Explore Pudong Financial District
 
Walk around the Lujiazui area and admire Shanghai's futuristic architecture.
 
### Key Landmarks
 
#### Shanghai Tower
- Third tallest building in the world at the time of filming
- Observation Deck Ticket: 180 Yuan
 
#### Shanghai World Financial Center
- Nicknamed the "Bottle Opener"
- Distinctive rectangular opening at the top
 
#### Jin Mao Tower
- Another iconic skyscraper in the district
 
---
 
# Shopping Stop 1
 
## 11:00 AM – AP Plaza Market
 
### Location
Shanghai Science and Technology Museum Metro Station (Line 2)
 
AP Plaza is an underground shopping market similar to Delhi's Palika Bazaar.
 
### What You Can Buy
- Bags
- Watches
- Shoes
- Clothing
- Souvenirs
- Accessories
 
### Shopping Tips
- Bargaining is essential
- Many products are replicas
- Start negotiations at 30–40% of quoted prices
- Mornings generally offer the best bargains
 
Suggested time: 2–3 hours
 
---
 
## 01:30 PM – Return to Hotel
 
Drop off shopping bags and take a short break before continuing.

# Shopping Stop 2
 
## 02:30 PM – Qipu Wholesale Clothing Market
 
One of Shanghai's largest wholesale clothing markets.
 
### Products Available

## 05:00 PM – Warehouse Restaurant
 
A social-media-famous restaurant located near People's Square.
 
### Why Visit
- Futuristic interiors
- Unique architecture
- Instagram- and TikTok-worthy décor
 
### Note for Vegetarians
Food options may be limited. The venue is better known for its atmosphere and drinks than vegetarian dining.

---
 
# Yu Garden District
 
## 06:00 PM – Yu Garden Area
 
One of Shanghai's most famous cultural and historical destinations.
 
### Attractions
- Traditional Chinese architecture
- Souvenir shops
- Classical-style buildings
- Traditional eateries
- Cultural art displays
 
### Important Note
The actual Yu Garden closes around 5:00 PM, so arrive earlier if you want to explore inside the garden itself.
 
---
 
## 07:00 PM – Explore Yu Garden at Night
 
The district becomes particularly beautiful after sunset.
 
### Highlights
- Illuminated lanterns
- Traditional Chinese architecture
- Historic atmosphere
- Local handicrafts
- Street photography opportunities
 
### Historical Background
Yu Garden was originally built during the Ming Dynasty by a government official for his parents. The name "Yu" symbolizes peace and comfort.
 
Many visitors consider this one of the most beautiful areas in Shanghai.

# Bund Night Walk
 
## 08:30 PM – Walk Along the Bund
 
Experience Shanghai's most iconic waterfront district.
 
### Highlights
- Colonial-era architecture
- Illuminated skyline
- Riverfront promenade
- Night photography
 
### Historical Context
The Bund became an important trading district after the Opium Wars and later evolved into one of China's most important financial and commercial areas.
 
Today's Shanghai skyline reflects decades of rapid urban development beginning in the 1990s.
 
Spend approximately one hour exploring the area.
