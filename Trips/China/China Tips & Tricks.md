## Before Arriving to China:

1. CCC certified charger.



## HSR

1. Book high speed rail (HSR) from trip.com
2. Tickets are linked with passport, just scan passport and sit on train.


## Within City Travel:

1. Alipay QR code scan
2. Take a card from Klook; China T - Union Card
3. Snack convenient store for buying bottled water.

## Must Install Apps:

1. VPNs
2. Alipay (Tourist friendly, easy acivation) & WeChat
3. Navigation: AMAP --> shows public transportation also
4. Taxi: DIDI; there is standalone app as well as its available in both alipay & weChat
5. Attractions, Hotels, Trains, Buses: Trip.com
6. Klook 2nd best option for attractions
7. In China every resturant offer some kind of coupon using `Dianpin` app.
8. Bike rental: 1Yuan per hour: Alipay can be used for this
